# StudySync (Lightweight Edition)

A streamlined study companion app that combines a focus timer with social features. Stay focused, earn coins, share moments through stories, and compete with friends on the leaderboard.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Focus Timer** – Start a study session and earn **coins** for every second you focus. Stop and collect rewards.
- **Stories** – Share photos from your camera or gallery as 24‑hour stories. View who has seen your stories.
- **Friends** – Add friends by username, accept/reject requests, and see your friend list.
- **Leaderboard** – Compete with your friends based on total coins earned.
- **Profile & Settings** – Customize your display name, profile picture, and change your password.
- **Authentication** – Sign up / log in with email and password, with password reset support.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 18 (UMD), Tailwind CSS, Babel standalone
- **Backend**: Firebase (Auth, Firestore)
- **Image Hosting**: Cloudinary (unsigned upload preset)
- **Format**: Single HTML file – no build step required.

---

## 🖥️ Usage

1. Locate apk/studysync.apk
2. Download it
3. Install it on your Android device and off you go!
